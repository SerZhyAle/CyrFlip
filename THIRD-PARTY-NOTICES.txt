0.10); CyrFlip applies colour and scale at draw time only.

## Outfit and Plus Jakarta Sans (website typefaces)

- **What:** the two typefaces of the GitHub Pages site (`docs/`), served from the site itself as WOFF2 files in
  `docs/assets/fonts/` (`docs/style.css` `@font-face`), so a visit to the site contacts no font host. The app does not
  use them.
- **Authors:** Outfit - The Outfit Project Authors; Plus Jakarta Sans - The Plus Jakarta Sans Project Authors
  (Tokotype)
- **Source:** <https://github.com/Outfitio/Outfit-Fonts>, <https://github.com/tokotype/PlusJakartaSans>
- **Licence:** SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 - <https://openfontlicense.org>. The full licence texts are
  `docs/assets/fonts/OFL-Outfit.txt` and `docs/assets/fonts/OFL-PlusJakartaSans.txt`. The files are the unmodified
  subsets that the fonts' public distribution (Google Fonts) serves; the fonts are not sold on their own.
