## What this repository must do to stay conformant

- **Third parties appear in text or as their own mark only** - no redrawn logos.
- **Scenario icons are shown as the OS reports them** (`LauncherIconResolver`): the program's own icon, a
  script's interpreter icon, and CyrFlip's own icon as the fallback - never a blank task.
- **Fallbacks come from the vocabulary**, and any vendored third-party glyph ships with its licence notice.
